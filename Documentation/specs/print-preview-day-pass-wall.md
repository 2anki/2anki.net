## Spec: Confidence before commit — free print preview, day pass first on the mobile limit wall

### Trio synthesis
- PM: two 15 Oct refunds share one shape, the user paid before seeing the outcome; build a free preview on `/print` and a day-pass-first limit wall for mobile one-off users, read success on the events table, never on refund count (below the power threshold).
- Designer: preview the first 3 cards through the real PDF renderer as an inline image (no watermark, audio placeholders visible), move the payment ask below it and lead that ask with a day pass; on the wall, the mechanism already exists (`LimitWall order='passes-first'` with the Day Pass featured) and the targeting must be computed server-side.
- Engineer: `/print` posts straight to the quota-gated `POST /api/apkg/pdf`, so a preview needs its own un-metered route plus a page-capped or screenshot render in `PdfRenderService`; the "pricing variant flag" is a random localStorage bucket used only by `/pricing`, there is no `sessions` table (dropped 2024-09-01), and one-off history has to come from `events` or `jobs` through `GET /api/users/locals`.
- Agreement: preview = first 3 cards, rendered by the PDF path, free for everyone, never touching the print quota; wall order is a server decision exposed through locals, recorded on the existing paywall events as a variant label kept in parity with the server allowlist.
- Conflict: PM proposed extending the pricing A/B enum and reusing `usePricingOrderVariant`; designer and engineer showed that flag is device-blind and history-blind and would mix with the live pricing-page test. Resolved: the wall gets its own server-computed order; the only enum touch is a new allowlisted label so checkout metadata and events can name the arm.
- Resulting plan: two parts, shipped as two PRs off this spec (part 1 first), each with its own usage event and a T+30 review issue.

**Outcome**: Part 1: no free user commits their monthly print, or a purchase, without first seeing how their deck renders. Read as `print_preview_shown` → PDF download or pass checkout on the events table, weekly; target: at least half of previews that end in a download or purchase, and every print refund request after launch names something the preview could not have shown. Part 2: mobile users classified as one-off see the day pass first; read as the pass share of `paywall_pass_clicked` + `paywall_upgrade_clicked` from `surface: 'limit-wall'` for the arm, weekly, against the reachable population (mobile free users who hit the cap and match the segment). Target: day-pass share for the arm at least 20 points above control. Refund rate is not a success metric: at about two relevant refunds per window a 30% move can be neither shown nor falsified.

**Goal alignment**: "Beautiful flashcards, fast and easy" on the trust axis: the user sees the beautiful (or broken) output before paying. Metrics: `print_preview_shown` conversion and limit-wall plan mix, both in the `events` table (`src/services/events/track.ts`), reviewed at T+30 from each part's merge.

**Problem**: On `/print`, selecting an `.apkg` posts the whole file to `POST /api/apkg/pdf`, which runs `printQuotaMiddleware` (free users: one PDF a month, then 402) and renders the entire deck. The user learns whether the deck prints acceptably only after the commit. On 15 Oct a buyer paid for a print before knowing whether the deck would print. Separately, `/limit` hardcodes `WALL_ORDER = 'unlimited-first'` (`web/src/pages/LimitPage/LimitPage.tsx`), so on a phone the first card below the headline is the subscription; a mobile user who needed one day was steered to a long plan the same day.

**Riskiest assumption**: that these two refunds are recurring patterns, not two anecdotes, and for part 1 that three cards faithfully predict the full PDF (pagination, media at scale, long cards can still disappoint after a clean preview).

**Smallest test**: before building part 2, count mobile limit-wall pass and subscription purchases refunded within 48 h over the last 90 days (Stripe refunds joined to `checkout_completed` events with `surface: 'limit-wall'`); if the reachable segment is under about 20 purchases a month the experiment cannot read a plan-mix shift and part 2 waits. Part 1 ships regardless as a trust improvement; its smallest test is a dev preview route with an audio-heavy deck and a 1000-card deck, checked by eye against the paid PDF before the route is wired to `/print`.

**What this removes**: Part 1 removes the blind commit: today the only action after file-select is the quota-gated generate. The preview becomes the first state and the charge moves behind a second explicit action. No toggle. Part 2 removes the single hardcoded wall order for the mobile one-off segment. No user-facing setting.

**Primary action**: `/print`: see how your deck prints, then download the full PDF. `/limit`: pick the plan that fits this session.

**Default behavior**: `/print`: every user who selects an `.apkg` sees the first 3 cards rendered exactly as the PDF would, free, before any quota check or charge; download is a separate action. `/limit`: a mobile user classified as one-off history sees passes first with the day pass featured; everyone else sees today's unlimited-first order. Chosen for the user, no prompt.

**Surface vocabulary**: Part 1 reuses the DownloadsPage `.apkg` card preview styling and `/print`'s own status line (`print.statusFree` / `print.statusUsed`) and notification states (`print.corrupted` / `print.genericError`). The render reuses `ExportApkgToPdfUseCase.buildHtml` and `ApkgPreviewService.getCardsPage(..., { print: true })`, not the flip-card viewer in `PreviewApkgPage` (one side at a time, would not reassure a printer). Part 2 reuses `LimitWall order='passes-first'` with `PassCards featureDayPass` and the `/dev/limit-preview` route that already shows both orders.

**Scope**:
- In, part 1: `POST /api/apkg/pdf/preview` in `src/routes/ApkgRouter.ts` behind `RequireAllowedOrigin`, `RequireAuthentication` and `ipRateLimit`, without `printQuotaMiddleware` and never calling `incrementPrintUsage`; a controller method on `ApkgController`; `ExportApkgToPdfUseCase.preview(buffer, options, 3)` reusing `parse`, `getCardsPage(parsed, 0, 3, '', null, { print: true })`, `buildHtml` and `PdfAssets`; a `PdfRenderService.renderPreviewImage` that returns a PNG of the first page (Puppeteer `page.screenshot` under the existing CSP, nonce and `guardLocalFiles`); a preview state in `PrintForm.tsx` with the inline image, caption, and the right-sized action below it; the `print_preview_shown` event in `web/src/lib/analytics/events.ts` and `src/types/AnalyticsEvents.ts`; a `/dev/print-preview` route (DEV only) showing the five states; new keys in all 10 locales.
- In, part 2: a server classifier use case (`src/usecases/users/ClassifyUsageHistoryUseCase.ts` over `events` or `jobs`) exposed as `limitWallOrder` on `GET /api/users/locals`; `LimitPage.tsx` reads it instead of the constant; a new allowlisted label `day-first` in `VALID_PRICING_VARIANTS` and the web `VARIANTS` (parity test), passed as `variant` to `paywall_shown`, `paywall_pass_clicked`, `paywall_upgrade_clicked` and `startPassCheckout(kind, variant, 'limit-wall')`; `limit.upgradeSubheadingPasses` in all 10 locales.
- Out: self-serve refunds; self-serve downgrades; preview on the upload or convert flow; day-pass-first on desktop; any change to pass or plan pricing or definitions; the native iOS paywall (`Laer-Smart/2anki.app`).

**User story**: As a free user printing an existing deck, I want to see the first cards exactly as they will print before I spend my monthly print or pay, so I do not pay for a PDF that is not what I expected. As a mobile learner hitting the card cap mid-session, I want the single-day option shown first when my history is short one-off sessions, so I buy what fits the job.

### Design notes
- Preview: first 3 cards, rendered through the real PDF table (`Front | Back`), shown inline as an image that fits the column; no watermark (the 3-card cap is the gate), full fidelity for images and math, audio and video as the PDF's own placeholders. Caption states it is a preview and that page breaks follow the chosen paper size. Preview renders once on drop; option changes show "Update preview".
- Action below the preview is right-sized to a one-off task: paying or free-available → "Download the full PDF"; free-used → "Get a day pass to print the whole deck" (day-pass checkout, `surface: 'print'`), tertiary "See all plans". Never show a payment ask after a failed or empty preview.
- Wall: `passes-first` order for the segment: headline (unchanged, protected copy) → "Keep converting today. Pay once — no subscription." → "Pay once — no subscription" with Day (featured) → Week → Semester → "Skip the monthly card cap" with the subscription → "Back to upload". Control is untouched. Keep the shared Day Pass badge; do not fork a per-surface badge.
- Visuals are settled on `/dev/limit-preview` (exists) and a new `/dev/print-preview` showing free-available, free-used, a 1–2 card deck, an audio-heavy deck and the render-failed state.
